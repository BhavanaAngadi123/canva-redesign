# Canva Redesign

An interactive concept prototype reimagining **Canva** (the design platform) — rebuilt from scratch with a 5-year horizon, designed for Gen Alpha creators, with AI as a co-creator rather than an autopilot.

## What it is

A clickable, animated prototype of a forward-looking Canva: a mission-first home, a working concept studio with selectable creative directions and adjustable originality signals, six category-first future systems, a research-backed evidence view, and a side-by-side comparison — **redesign on the left, today's Canva on the right** — with filters.

Every proposed feature was novelty-checked against similar applications (Adobe Express, Figma, Microsoft Designer, Pinterest, Kittl, and others): anything those apps already have was cut or reworked.

## Why

Today's Canva serves 260M+ monthly users, but faces a growing "Canva look" — template sameness that AI features risk accelerating. This redesign attacks originality head-on while keeping the approachability that made Canva huge.

## View it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser — no build step, no dependencies. It's a single self-contained file.

## Research basis

Built on deep research into today's Canva (features, pricing, complaints), Canva Inc.'s 2025–2026 direction (Magic Studio, Affinity, enterprise and education pushes), competitor moves, Gen Alpha creator behavior, and credible 2026–2031 design-tool trends.
