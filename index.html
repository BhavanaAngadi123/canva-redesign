Future bets

Six clean breaks from the category.

These are the concepts for which the research found no close equivalent across Adobe Express, Figma, Microsoft Designer, Kittl, VistaCreate or Pinterest.

01 / Multiplayer design rooms

Create like it’s game night.

Voice, reactions, live remix battles and shared goals turn collaboration from review work into social play. Today’s design collaboration is built like an office; this is built like a world.

BAYou · art direction
MKMika · type+1 rhythm
JOJo · motion
AITaste coach · listening
02 / Design missions

A skill tree replaces the toolbar.

Creative quests reveal capabilities as creators need them. Progress tracks judgment—hierarchy, pacing, contrast—not button clicks.

03 / Spatial by default

From flat post to real space.

Everyday creators can turn a design into a product turntable, room preview or AR object without leaving the main canvas.

04 / Remix economy

Influence becomes visible—and valuable.

Every fork carries its creative lineage. Original makers are credited automatically, and revenue can flow backward through the chain.

05 / Living designs

A design that knows when it changed.

Rules connect content, context and time: a campaign updates its facts, adapts its layout and records every revision. This is presented as a future product bet, not a working live-data feature here.

06 / Kid-safe creator wallet

Earn with visible guardrails.

Age-aware selling, parent visibility, spending controls and plain-language rights make monetization safer for young creators.
